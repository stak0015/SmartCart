US 5.3 (AC 5.3.1-5.3.3) — Record the Prices I Actually Saw
US 5.3（AC 5.3.1-5.3.3）——记录我实际看到的价格
==================================================

Scope / 范围
This note records how the shopping checklist was aligned with acceptance
criteria AC 5.3.1, AC 5.3.2, and AC 5.3.3.
本文档记录购物清单与验收标准 AC 5.3.1、AC 5.3.2、AC 5.3.3 的对齐情况。

--------------------------------------------------

AC 5.3.1 — Record the price I actually saw / 记录实际看到的价格

对齐内容 / Alignment target
- Purchased 行可录入/编辑实际单价（RM），须为正数数值、显示两位小数；
  非法值给出可理解的报错。
- A line marked Purchased accepts an actual unit price in RM; it must be
  positive, numeric, shown to two decimals; bad values are rejected with an
  understandable error.

对齐所作的修改 / Changes made for alignment
- 行模型新增 actualPriceRm 字段；仅 Purchased 行可在编辑对话框中录入、
  修改或清空实际单价。
- A new actualPriceRm field was added to the line model; only Purchased
  lines offer an "Actual unit price (RM)" entry in the edit dialog.
- 录入值须为正数、最多两位小数，输入即校验并复用既有双语报错；留空视为
  "未知"而非错误。
- Entries must be positive with at most two decimals, validated as typed with
  the existing bilingual error; a blank entry means "unknown", not an error.

--------------------------------------------------

AC 5.3.2 — My price kept separate from the official price / 实际价与官方价分开

对齐内容 / Alignment target
- 实际价标注 "Shopper recorded"，原 PriceCatcher 或参考价并列保留；编辑
  实际价绝不改写官方价、推荐小计或商店排名。
- The actual price is labelled "Shopper recorded" while the original
  PriceCatcher or reference value stays visible separately; editing it never
  overwrites the official price, the recommendation subtotal, or the store
  ranking.

对齐所作的修改 / Changes made for alignment
- 实际价存于独立字段 actualPriceRm，与官方/参考价在行上并列显示，行尾
  标注 "Shopper recorded"（马来语 "Dicatat oleh pembeli"）。
- The actual price lives in the separate actualPriceRm field and renders
  alongside the official/reference price with a "Shopper recorded" label
  (Malay: "Dicatat oleh pembeli").
- 实际价相关计算全部为本地纯函数，官方价、计划小计与推荐数据不被触碰，
  并有隔离测试守护。
- All actual-price computation is local pure functions; the official price,
  planned subtotal, and recommendation data are never touched, guarded by
  isolation tests.

--------------------------------------------------

AC 5.3.3 — Actual line totals / 实际行小计

对齐内容 / Alignment target
- 有实际单价与数量时行小计即时重算（价 × 数量）；实际价清空或未知时
  行小计留空，绝不显示 RM0.00。
- With an actual unit price and purchased quantity, the line total
  recalculates immediately (price × quantity); when the actual price is
  cleared or unknown, the line total stays empty — never RM0.00.

对齐所作的修改 / Changes made for alignment
- 新增派生函数 actualLineTotalRm：有实际价时按 价 × 数量 即时重算；
  实际价为 null 时返回 null，界面渲染为空白（绝不 RM0.00）。
- A derived actualLineTotalRm recomputes price × quantity immediately; a
  null actual price yields null and renders blank (never RM0.00).
- 实际价随清单本地持久化；存储信封版本升至 v2，旧版已存清单经迁移链
  自动补齐字段，不丢数据。
- The actual price persists locally with the checklist; the storage envelope
  rises to v2 and older saved checklists are upgraded by the migration chain
  without data loss.
