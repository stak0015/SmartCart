US 4i.3–4i.6 README - Per-item festival estimates, recommendations, and early-purchase savings
US 4i.3–4i.6 交付说明 - 逐商品节庆估价、采购推荐与提前采购节省
================================================================

1. What this change does / 本次交付内容
----------------------------------------------------------------
中文：
- 新增逐商品节庆估价接口：
  GET /api/festivals/{festival_id}/items?state={state}
- 完整数据商品使用自身 baseline/peak 窗口，展示预计价格区间、历史涨幅、
  历史窗口、观察数和样本天数。
- 价格数据不完整的商品在存在当前中位价时，按节日-州属平均涨价比例
  换算预计区间，并显示 Estimated from the festival average rise。
- 无可用基准价时返回 unavailable，不编造价格。
- 新增历史涨幅 Top 10 推荐：
  GET /api/festivals/{festival_id}/top-items?state={state}&limit=10
- 推荐只取完整样本、正涨幅的逐商品统计；当前价使用跨店中位补值；
  节庆特有关键商品显示独立标记。
- 推荐项可通过既有购物篮加入，数量为 1；没有 catalogue item_id 或
  当前价时禁用加入按钮。
- 新增提前采购节省核算：
  POST /api/festivals/early-purchase-savings
- 提前采购节省只使用历史同期价格；购买日期必须在对应节前涨价窗口内，
  且成交单价低于历史同期均价时才计入。
- Shopping history 单独显示 Early-purchase savings 总额和逐记录金额，
  不与常规比价节省合并。
- estimated_price_method_version=4i.6.1-v1；不修改 PostgreSQL schema，
  不持久化购买明细。

English:
- Adds the per-item festival estimate endpoint:
  GET /api/festivals/{festival_id}/items?state={state}
- Items with complete history use their own baseline/peak window and show the
  expected price range, historical rise percentage, history window, observation
  count, and observed days.
- Incomplete items with a current median price use the festival-state average
  rise ratio and are labelled "Estimated from the festival average rise".
- Items without a usable baseline return unavailable; no price is invented.
- Adds historical top-ten recommendations:
  GET /api/festivals/{festival_id}/top-items?state={state}&limit=10
- Recommendations use complete-sample, positive item-level rises only. Current
  price uses the cached cross-store median, and festival-specific items carry a
  separate badge.
- A recommendation can be added to the existing basket with quantity 1. Add is
  disabled when the catalogue item id or current price is unavailable.
- Adds early-purchase savings:
  POST /api/festivals/early-purchase-savings
- Only historical same-period prices are eligible. A purchase counts only when
  its date is inside the festival-state rise window and its paid unit price is
  below the historical average.
- Shopping history shows a separate Early-purchase savings total and per-record
  amount; it is never merged into ordinary comparison savings.
- estimated_price_method_version=4i.6.1-v1. No PostgreSQL schema change and no
  purchase-detail persistence.

4. Additional product-surface ACs / 新增商品界面 AC
----------------------------------------------------------------
中文：
- AC 4i.3.5：商品选择卡片在生效节前窗口内显示节日名称、预计上涨百分比范围、预计价格范围和提前购买提醒。
- AC 4i.3.6：结算侧栏新增独立的“提前购买预计可省”，按 max(0, 预计最高价 - 实际成交单价) × 数量计算；仅门店实际价格参与，不与常规比价节省合并。
- AC 4i.3.7：默认商品列表和搜索结果按匹配度、门店价格覆盖数、中位价可用性、商品名排序；无价格商品置底。

English:
- AC 4i.3.5: the product selection card shows the festival name, expected rise percentage range, expected price range, and an early-purchase reminder while the pre-festival window is active.
- AC 4i.3.6: checkout shows a separate estimated early-purchase saving calculated as max(0, forecast maximum price - actual store unit price) x quantity. Only actual store prices participate, and the amount is not merged with comparison savings.
- AC 4i.3.7: default product browsing and search results are ordered by match tier, store-price coverage, median-price availability, and item name. Items without prices appear last.
