============================================================
US 7.3 README — Understand why an alternative is recommended
                理解替代项的推荐理由（营养对比）
============================================================

1. What this commit does / 本次提交内容
------------------------------------------------------------
[zh]
为每张 "Healthier alternatives" 卡片增加独立的 "Why this alternative?" 动作
（与"打开该替代项"的主区域分离），并按下述方式展开内联营养对比：
- 在替代区下方内联展开，不打开其他物品、不改当前数量、不改购物篮；
- 标识被替换的原物品与所选替代项；
- 两侧数值统一在同一比较基准（per 100 g / per 100 mL）并标明单位；
- 标明两侧的营养数据集与映射条目（数据集名 + 记录号 + 条目描述）；
- 当另一项受跟踪营养素对替代项不利时，显式给出 trade-off，不暗示"样样更好"；
- 缺失或不可比的值分别标为 "Unavailable" / "Non-comparable"，绝不当作 0；
- 若该映射使用的是通用食物条目，明确披露 "Generic food mapping ..."；
- "Close insights" 可收起对比，当前物品、数量与购物篮保持不变；
- 点击另一条建议的 "Why this alternative?" 时，对比切换为与该新建议的比较。

同时更新已审核的替代项数据：按"有价优先（至少 5 家店报价）→ 中位观测价由低到高取前 2"
为每个产品族给出替代项，产品族内无任何有价候选时保留原无价替代作为兜底。这样同一物品
可以有两条替代建议，切换对比才有可比较的对象。

[en]
Adds a separate "Why this alternative?" action to each "Healthier alternatives"
card (independent of the card area that opens the item) and expands an inline
nutrition comparison:
- it expands below the alternatives section without opening another item, and
  without changing the current quantity selection or the basket;
- it identifies the original item and the selected alternative;
- both sides are shown on one shared basis (per 100 g / per 100 mL) with units;
- it names the nutrition dataset and mapped entry for both sides (dataset name,
  record code and entry description);
- when another tracked nutrient is worse for the alternative, the trade-off is
  shown rather than implying the alternative improves everything;
- missing or incompatible values are labelled "Unavailable" / "Non-comparable"
  and are never treated as zero;
- a generic food mapping is disclosed explicitly;
- "Close insights" collapses the comparison and leaves the current item,
  quantity selection and basket unchanged;
- selecting "Why this alternative?" on another suggestion switches the
  comparison to that suggestion.

It also updates the reviewed alternative data: for each product family the
alternatives are the two lowest median-priced candidates that carry at least
5 official price observations (priced-first), keeping the previous unpriced
alternative as a fallback when a family has no priced candidate. This is what
gives one item two suggestions to switch between.

2. How to verify / 验证方式
------------------------------------------------------------
[zh]
1) 打开 Shop 页，搜索 "SEBATIAN"，打开 "BLENDED COOKING OIL BRAND HELANG"。
   预期：出现两张替代卡片；点第一张的 "Why this alternative?"，面板显示
   "BLENDED COOKING OIL BRAND HELANG → VECORN CORN OIL"；再点第二张的
   同一按钮，面板切换为 "→ DAISY CORN OIL"。
2) 搜索 "krimer"，打开 "SWEETENED CREAMER BERVITAMIN BRAND F&N"，点
   "Why this alternative?"。
   预期：显示 "Comparison basis: per 100 g"、带单位的营养表、两侧
   "Nutrition source"，以及 "Saturated fat: Unavailable"（不是 0）。
3) 点 "Close insights"。
   预期：面板收起，物品、数量与购物篮不变。
4) 搜索 "LEMBU"，打开任一本牛肉，点 "Why this alternative?"。
   预期：显示 "Generic food mapping — the nutrition entry describes a generic
   food, not this exact product."。
5) 后端测试：cd backend && .venv/Scripts/python -m pytest
   前端测试：cd frontend && npx pnpm test

[en]
1) Open the Shop page, search "SEBATIAN" and open
   "BLENDED COOKING OIL BRAND HELANG".
   Expected: two alternative cards; select "Why this alternative?" on the first
   and the panel reads
   "BLENDED COOKING OIL BRAND HELANG → VECORN CORN OIL"; select the same action
   on the second and the panel switches to "→ DAISY CORN OIL".
2) Search "krimer", open "SWEETENED CREAMER BERVITAMIN BRAND F&N" and select
   "Why this alternative?".
   Expected: "Comparison basis: per 100 g", a nutrient table with units,
   "Nutrition source" for both sides, and "Saturated fat: Unavailable" (not 0).
3) Select "Close insights".
   Expected: the panel collapses; the item, quantity and basket are unchanged.
4) Search "LEMBU", open any beef item and select "Why this alternative?".
   Expected: "Generic food mapping — the nutrition entry describes a generic
   food, not this exact product.".
5) Backend tests: cd backend && .venv/Scripts/python -m pytest
   Frontend tests: cd frontend && npx pnpm test
