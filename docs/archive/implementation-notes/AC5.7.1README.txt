AC 5.7.1 README — Export controls
=================================

本次提交内容 / What this commit does
------------------------------------
购物清单新增导出入口：清单非空时，底部操作区出现 Export checklist 按钮
（Record shopping trip 与 Delete checklist 之间），点击弹出无障碍对话框，
提供 Download as image 与 Download as PDF 两个选项及取消按钮。对话框
遵循应用既有模式：aria 关联、打开时初始焦点落在第一个选项、Esc 或取消
关闭后焦点归还 Export 按钮。清单为空时导出按钮禁用，并显示说明文字
"Add an item to your checklist before exporting."。全部文案支持英文与
Bahasa Melayu 双语。两个下载动作的实际文件生成将在后续提交接通，本次
提交为导出入口与交互形态。

Adds the export entry point to the shopping checklist: when the checklist
is non-empty, an Export checklist button appears in the bottom action area
(between Record shopping trip and Delete checklist), opening an accessible
dialog that offers Download as image and Download as PDF plus a cancel
button. The dialog follows the app's established patterns: aria wiring,
initial focus on the first option, and focus returned to the Export button
on Escape or cancel. When the checklist is empty, the export button is
disabled with the note "Add an item to your checklist before exporting."
All copy is bilingual (English and Bahasa Melayu). The actual file
generation behind the two download actions lands in a later commit; this
commit covers the export entry and its interaction shape.
