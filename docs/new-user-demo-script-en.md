# SmartCart Screen-Recording Script (New-User Style, English)

> How to use: open the website, hit record, and follow the lines below. Each part is short — if you stumble, just keep going.
> Stay in character the whole time: you have NEVER seen this website before. Keep it to 3–5 minutes total.

---

## Opening (10 seconds)

> Okay, so today I'm trying out a website I've never used before. Apparently it helps you find the cheapest supermarket for your groceries. Let's see how it works.

(open the website)

## Finding items (about 1 minute)

> Alright, first things first — I need to find stuff to buy. Let me try searching for chicken.

(type "ayam" in the search box)

> Oh nice, a bunch of chicken options came up. This one, "AYAM BERSIH", one kilo — looks like a normal everyday item. I'll add one to my basket.

(click + Add to basket)

> Now let me search for rice.

(type "beras" and add one item)

> Okay, I think two items is enough for today.

## Checking the basket (about 30 seconds)

(click the basket icon at the top right)

> Here I can see what I've picked so far. The quantity says one — let me bump it up to two.

(click the + button)

> Looks good. Moving on.

## Setting location and travel (about 1 minute)

> Now it's asking where I am. I'll type Putrajaya... oh, there's a suggestion dropdown, I'll tap the first one.

(type Putrajaya, click the suggestion)

> Next, how am I getting there... I'll go by car. And for distance, let's say within 10 kilometres — don't want to drive too far.

(select Car, select 10 km)

> Let's find stores I can actually reach.

(click Find reachable stores)

## Reading the results (1–2 minutes)

> Okay, here's a list of stores. I'm guessing the first one is the best pick... let me take a look.

(pause, look at the numbers on the card)

> It's actually really clear: how much the groceries cost, how much the trip there and back costs, and the grand total. So it's not just about cheap items — it counts the petrol and travel too. That's a smart way to think about it.

(click Select store)

> On this page I can see the price of every single item at this store — unit price times quantity, line by line. That's easy to follow.

## Closing (10 seconds)

> Honestly, that was pretty smooth. No manual needed. If this really helps me find the cheapest place to shop, I'd definitely use it. That's it for me.

(stop recording)

---

## Quick tips

- Say the line, then do the action — don't wait in silence. If something confuses you, say so out loud ("hmm, I don't get this part") — that's exactly what we want to capture.
- You don't need to read every number out loud; one or two is enough, like "so around fifty ringgit in total".
- Don't refresh the page midway — your basket will empty and you'll have to start over.
